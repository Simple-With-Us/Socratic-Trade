# Exit-Strategy Intelligence: Expert Panel Findings

**Panel:** trader-risk, quant-ml, llm-prompt, exec-systems — four subsystem maps, four position papers, one adversarial debate round. **Synthesis basis:** all load-bearing claims below were re-verified against the working tree during synthesis (including the debate round's one major factual correction — see Gap 2). Effort scale: **S** ≈ ≤1 day, **M** ≈ 2–5 days, **L** ≈ 1–2+ weeks of single-agent work, each including tests.

---

## Verdict in one paragraph

Socratic-Trade's exit *plumbing* is genuinely excellent — idempotent synthetic fires with CAS claims and portable refIds (`synthetic-stops.ts:643-677`, `db-api-keys.ts:900-979`), lot-keyed stop-plan integrity (`db-api-keys.ts:1542-1584`), bracket teardown queues (`broker-protective-stops.ts:994-1043`) — but its exit *policy* is fire-and-forget: a stop plan is written once at fill, can never be revised without adding size (`sanitizeProposals` drops `stopPlan` on sell/cover, `strategy.ts:5195-5197`), is invisible to the LLM that re-decides exits every hour (`userContent` at `strategy.ts:4249-4364` carries no protection state), and is enforced through lanes with three silent tail holes: a bad-tick filter that permanently disarms trailing stops after a >10% gap (`synthetic-stops.ts:30,114,528`), fixed/ATR plans that have **no tick-cadence enforcement at all** (excluded from the synthetic monitor at `synthetic-stops.ts:406,440` — hourly proactive checks only), and long-only broker-held protection (`broker-protective-stops.ts:492-499`) on the eve of live shorts (owner decision 2026-07-10). The panel converged on a three-phase plan: **A)** make today's promises true (gap fix, halted-mode protection, prompt visibility, honest disclosures, options visibility); **B)** persist a parameterized Exit Contract and extend broker-resident lanes to ATR plans and shorts; **C)** add the hold-and-retune revision verb, structured invalidation/time exits, clamped numeric elicitation, and the counterfactual ledger that makes any of it measurable. Every new behavior ships as an owner-adjustable preference with a default and an off-switch — the `iraWashSaleHandling` template — never a cage.

## How exits work today

**At proposal time**, the Bull strategist emits per-trade `bracketStopLoss`/`bracketTakeProfit` prices and a `stopPlan` style (`default|fixed|atr|trailing|none` + rationale) on openings (`strategy.ts:4463-4479, 4541-4542`). It cannot set numeric parameters (ATR multiple, trail %, fixed %) — those come from account `riskRules` (`types.ts:586-612`); there is no invalidation field on a normal opening (only on `autonomyOverride`, `strategy.ts:4450`) and no time-based exit field anywhere (`holdingDays` is outcome-only, `types.ts:2139`). Nothing is ever rejected: `sanitizeProposals` (`strategy.ts:5150-5216`) drops/clamps, and `enrichOpeningProposal` (`strategy.ts:5254-5429`) backfills defaults, attaches broker brackets where supported (Alpaca/Tradier, ≥1 whole share), and — for explicit fixed/atr plans — **discards the LLM's own stop price and reprices from the account-pinned distance** so all enforcement layers agree (`strategy.ts:5343-5348`, PR #1371).

**After fill**, the surviving plan is persisted to `position_stop_plans` keyed by lot basis (`performance.ts:300-361`; `db.ts:2435-2446`) and enforced by a stack: broker-native brackets/trails (Alpaca `alpaca.ts:472-567`, Tradier OTOCO `tradier.ts:690-748`), broker-held GTC stops re-placed each tick by a reconciler (`broker-protective-stops.ts:210-992`), a ~60s synthetic trailing-stop monitor (`synthetic-stops.ts:147-754`, invoked `scheduler.ts:691-696`), hourly proactive full-exit proposals on stop breach (`generateProactiveRiskProposals`, `strategy.ts:5431-5551`), and deterministic take-profit trim bands (`strategy.ts:5600-5653`). Exits are structurally privileged in policy — sell/cover pass through halted/close_only/liquidating states and skip opening gates (`policy.ts:315-330`). The LLM re-reviews the full held book every cadence run (default 60 min) with rich context (`strategy.ts:4285-4363`) and may sell/trim any position (`strategy.ts:3940`).

**The four most important gaps:**

1. **Write-once, decided blind.** The stop plan is frozen at entry for the position's life — the LLM can only change it by scaling in (`strategy.ts:5195-5197`), the owner cannot change it per-position at all (no route/UI writes `position_stop_plans`; display-only, `app/console/components/positions.tsx:48`) — and the LLM re-decides exits every run without seeing the plan, the resting protection, or the distance-to-stop (verified: zero `stopPlan` references in `strategy-prompts.ts`; no protection field in `userContent`).
2. **Enforcement tail holes — with a corrected exposure map.** The debate round found that three of four papers mis-stated who the gap-deadlock bug hits. Verified truth: fixed/atr plans are *purged from and never registered in* the synthetic monitor (`synthetic-stops.ts:406, 440`) — their only intraday enforcement is the hourly proactive check plus any surviving entry bracket. So (a) an **atr-plan position on Tradier opened with a market entry has effectively no protection but an hourly check** (Tradier market entries silently drop the bracket, `tradier.ts:683-692`; Tradier has no broker-held stop lane, `broker-protective-stops.ts:97`); (b) the bad-tick deadlock (`synthetic-stops.ts:30,114,528` — a >10% gap makes every subsequent real print a "bad tick" forever, and a bad tick never updates `lastPrice`) disarms **trailing-plan and account-trail positions, including shorts** (`synthetic-stops.ts:443-444`), exactly when the stop matters most; (c) an unattended restart reverts `active`→`halted` (`scheduler.ts:274-318`, unless per-user `autoResumeOnBoot`) and `halted` skips the monitor entirely (`scheduler.ts:683-696` — "the only no-order state"), leaving only broker-held protection.
3. **Shorts are going live with the thinnest protection tier.** All broker-held stop lanes filter `quantity > 0` (`broker-protective-stops.ts:492-499`); a short whose entry bracket was stripped (`stopPlan:"trailing"/"none"` strip both legs, `strategy.ts:5293-5296`) is protected only by the app-uptime synthetic lane — which carries gap #2 — against an unbounded-loss instrument.
4. **Elicitation contradicts execution, and some positions are invisible.** The prompt demands setup-derived, ATR-scaled stops ("Do NOT default every trade to the same fixed percentage," `strategy-prompts.ts:149`) while the candidate evidence contains **no ATR field** (verified: `compactCandidateForPrompt`, `strategy.ts:5012-5052`) and fixed/atr plans reprice the LLM's stop away anyway (`strategy.ts:5343-5348`); the prompt also instructs shorts to emit `shortStopLossPct` (`strategy-prompts.ts:105`) — a policy field the schema cannot express. Meanwhile OCC option positions are deliberately filtered out of the book (`tradier.ts:499-506`), so an assigned or manually-opened contract is invisible to every exit layer.

## Consensus recommendations (post-debate, ranked)

*Unanimous (all four papers, all four critiques). The only outright bug on the board.*
**What/why:** (a) Replace single-print rejection in `evaluateStop` with confirmation-based acceptance: persist `suspect_price`/`suspect_count` on the synthetic-stop row (state must survive tick restarts — in-memory is not enough); N=2–3 consecutive out-of-band prints agreeing within ~1.5–2% accept the new level, update `lastPrice`, and evaluate the stop; add a session-boundary reset at the regular-hours open. **Debate amendment (trader):** during extended hours, acceptance must be corroborated by a real (non-synthetic) bid/ask — the quote plumbing already distinguishes these (`synthetic-stops.ts:516-520`) — so two thin 4 a.m. prints can't fire a marketable-limit into a chasm. Note the DB `lastPrice` fallback (`:495-506`) cannot corroborate — it replays the stale anchor itself (exec correction). (b) Run the monitor in `halted` in protective-only mode behind an owner preference (`protectWhileHalted`, default on): the suppression hook already exists (`running=false` → "exit suppressed" audit, `synthetic-stops.ts:532-534`); change `protectiveState` at `scheduler.ts:683-696` to include halted when the toggle is set. This decouples "resume trading" from "resume protection" — today `autoResumeOnBoot` (`scheduler.ts:277-298`) is the only lever and it re-arms *everything*.
**Sketch:** `synthetic-stops.ts` (`evaluateStop`, `:109-130`; upsert at `:528`), two columns in `db.ts` migrate + `db-api-keys.ts` upsert, `scheduler.ts:683-696`, a Guardrails toggle in `field-defs.ts`. Unit test replaying a −25% gap sequence and an extended-hours thin-print sequence. Because the scheduler comment calls halted "the only no-order state" deliberately, write the design note per CLAUDE.md before changing it.

## Contested points — rulings

**1. Who owns the stop number?** (trader: clamp and execute LLM numerics now; quant: shadow-log, grant authority at n≥30 positive delta; llm-prompt: elicit the derivation, recompute server-side; exec: nothing until the distance is persisted.)
**Ruling: derivation + clamps + live execution, strictly after the Exit Contract migration; no shadow-authority gate.** The shadow gate dies three deaths: at this account's trade rate n≥30 per comparison is a year during which the prompt/model rotates (the sample scores a generator that no longer exists); the comparison is confounded because stop width should drive position size, so replaying LLM widths against rule-sized positions measures the wrong thing; and the prompt either admits the numbers don't execute (models under-invest in dead fields) or lies. Owner-set clamp bands already bound the worst case to the band edge. But trader's original "honor the numbers in the enricher" is equally rejected as written — it recreates the exact layer-divergence bug PR #1371 fixed (verified comment at `strategy.ts:5343-5348`: every layer prices off the same distance). The safe order is: persist (Rec 3) → elicit `stopBasis` → recompute in-band → write the resolved distance where all layers read it → log everything to the ledger regardless.

**2. Event response: full `runStrategyOnce` vs a scoped single-position review?** (trader/quant: full runs, caps exist; llm-prompt: scoped call for cost and action-space containment.)
**Ruling: phase it — full runs now, scoped review as the end state, built as a *mode* of `runStrategyOnce`, never a parallel pipeline.** The budget objection to full runs is weaker than claimed (llm-budget is real and enforced), so turn the engine on without waiting. But llm-prompt's action-space point stands: an 8-K-triggered full run re-opens the entire entry space at moments of market stress, and full-run cost is why the engine has sat dark since it was built. The scoped mode must share the per-account run lock and the duplicate-exit guard (exec's non-negotiable — overlapping cadence and triggered runs on one symbol double-exit otherwise), share sanitize→policy→placement, include a three-line portfolio summary (trader: a single-position reviewer that can't see concentration or shared factor exposure is dangerous), include the entry-time thesis/regime snapshot, and forbid entries.

**3. Invalidation: prose string vs typed trigger array with a deterministic price watcher?**
**Ruling: string + `maxHoldingDays` in v1; typed `event`/`regime_flip` kinds in v2 wired to the trigger engine; price-kind watchers never.** Trader and quant convincingly killed the price watcher: a price-expressible invalidation *is a stop*, and a second price-trigger machine beside the stop system — different debounce, different idempotency history, same instrument — is precisely the MU-incident failure class. But llm-prompt's counterpunch also lands: purely prose invalidations re-import sycophancy at review time ("guidance was *soft*, not *cut*"). Resolution: time is evaluated deterministically (it can't race a price); event/regime kinds are matched by the already-wired producers so a fired trigger arrives in the prompt as a **fact** ("TRIGGER FIRED: …"), not a question; anything price-shaped is folded into `stopPlan`/`stopBasis`.

**4. Are exit revisions risk-neutral?** (exec: yes, exempt from opening gates; trader: widening is risk-additive in dollars-at-stop.)
**Ruling: asymmetric, trader is right.** Tighten = risk-reducing, auto-eligible, exit-privileged. Widen/disarm = risk-adding: it increases book heat that nothing currently re-checks (heat is computed at entry sizing only, verified `strategy-risk.ts:660-673`), so widening re-enters the heat math, surfaces incremental $-at-stop and new R on the card, defaults to propose, and offers the paired trim to hold $-risk constant. Per the owner philosophy, both directions are settable to auto — the default is a preference, the accounting is not optional.

**5. Persisted contract vs live re-derivation; what does a Guardrails edit mean?**
**Ruling: the persisted row is the contract of record; the synthetic trail keeps ratcheting live; account-level Guardrails edits apply to new lots by default with an explicit "apply to existing positions" action.** Exec's "all layers read persisted only" silently changes today's semantics (an owner who tightens the account stop expects the book to tighten); quant/trader's amendment — make the scope choice explicit, never silent in either direction — is the correct owner-facing shape. Log persisted-vs-live-recomputed distance per tick into `exit_events` so the stale-contract question gets answered empirically.

**6. Mandatory per-position review verdicts every run?** (llm-prompt: yes, with `wouldEnterToday`; trader: rubber-stamping + action bias; quant: hourly self-reports are autocorrelated non-signal.)
**Ruling: keep the probe, drop the cadence.** Require review verdicts (`hold|trim|exit|amend`, `thesisIntact`, `wouldEnterToday`) only on **trigger events and threshold crossings** (fired invalidation, new MAE low, +2R reached, earnings within 5 sessions), with batch "hold, unchanged" otherwise. Persist as telemetry; score verdict *changes* against outcomes; never feed verdict levels to the tuner. The `wouldEnterToday`-no-but-hold-anyway pattern is the disposition-effect signature worth catching — hourly forced verdicts would bury it in noise.

**7. Default for protection-while-halted?** (papers: default on; exec caveat: `halted` may mean "hands off, I'm trading this manually," and the scheduler comment marks the current behavior deliberate.)
**Ruling: ship the toggle default-ON for protective exits, but present it as an explicit semantic choice at enable time** ("halted = no new risk" vs "halted = fully disarmed"), with the note that live exit-order coverage already suppresses double-firing against the owner's own working orders (`synthetic-stops.ts:445-475`). Firing an owner-configured stop is executing the owner's standing instruction, not paternalism. Document the semantics change with a design note per the repo's paper-trail rule.

**Shorts — ~92% built, off-by-default, about to go live; protection tier is inverted.** Proposal/policy/accounting are real: double-gated sides (`strategy-risk.ts:91-94`, `policy.ts:433-459` — a short must declare a stop), notional/exposure caps, FIFO cover P&L (`performance.ts:484-520`), directional brackets (`strategy.ts:5361-5368`), synthetic low-watermark trail firing covers (`synthetic-stops.ts:443-444, 589`). **Options — invisible today; visibility first, trading later.** No order surface exists anywhere (roadmap:48-54) and positions are deliberately filtered from the book (`tradier.ts:499-506`), so an assigned or manually-opened contract has no stop, no P&L, no prompt presence, no UI row. ## What NOT to do

- **No second price-trigger machine.** Price-kind invalidation watchers running in the tick loop beside the stop system were killed in debate: two lanes reacting to the same price with different idempotency state is the MU-incident (~280 duplicate sells) failure class. Price-shaped invalidations are stops — encode them in `stopPlan`/`stopBasis`.
- **Do not honor LLM numeric stop params in `enrichOpeningProposal` before the contract is persisted.** It recreates the layer-divergence bug PR #1371 fixed (`strategy.ts:5343-5348`); the discard-and-reprice stays until Rec 3 lands.
- **No shadow-mode authority gate for LLM numerics.** Small-n never arrives, the sizing confound makes the comparison meaningless, and the prompt-honesty dilemma poisons the sample. Clamps + derivation + ledger instead.
- **No proposal generation from the tick loop.** It bypasses the run lock and duplicate-exit guard, spams approval cards under `propose`, and races the synthetic lane with different idempotency keys. Static synthetic rows (Rec 2) achieve the goal inside the proven framework.
- **Do not overload `OrderSide` with `"hold"` or a revision value.** `OrderSide` is a named cross-file consistency trap (CLAUDE.md); the sibling `exitRevisions[]` array is the only shape with zero blast radius on the order state machine.
- **No inline cancel-replace of protection from any handler.** Only the reconcile loop cancels resting protection (`broker-protective-stops.ts:486-490` pattern). Adopt as a repo rule.
- **No trailing-stop cells in the daily-OHLC counterfactual ledger.** Intra-bar HWM-vs-touch ordering is unknowable; a daily-bar trail counterfactual is bias dressed as data. Fixed/time/no-stop only until intraday bars exist.
- **No conditional-on-winners excursion rules in the tuner** ("winners' MAE was small → tighten"): survivorship-biased and self-reinforcing toward the whipsaw death spiral. Width evidence comes from full-population counterfactual replay, min-n gated and shrunk.
- **No mandatory hourly per-position verdicts.** Autocorrelated rubber-stamp telemetry plus induced action bias; event/threshold-triggered reviews only.
- **Do not auto-apply riskRules tuner changes.** The write-scope wall (`strategy-tuning.ts:1331-1334`) stays; the panel's work makes the *evidence* for manual approval good.
- **No per-position exit-only LLM call per tick,** and no LLM raw stop prices executing unclamped — cost-prohibitive and trust-inverted respectively; cadence + events + deterministic lanes is the right decomposition.
- **No take-profit "plans" symmetric to stop plans yet.** The deterministic banded trim ratchet (`strategy.ts:5600-5653`) is the best-designed exit component in the system; counterfactual-score it before adding LLM degrees of freedom.
- **No paternalistic hard blocks anywhere in this program.** Every gate above — halted protection, widen-requires-approval, time-stop behavior, trigger scopes, clamp bands — ships as an owner preference with a disclosed default and an off-switch, per the repo's binding product philosophy.
