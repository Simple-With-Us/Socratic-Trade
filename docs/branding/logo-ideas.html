Socratic-Trade · Brand exploration

Twelve marks for a market that answers questions

Every concept crosses the Socratic half of the name (question, dialogue, examination, Greek antiquity) with the trading half (candlesticks, trend lines, delta). All marks share one discipline: a single ink color plus the dashboard's emerald accent, so they sit natively in the existing UI on both light and dark grounds. Each preview shows the mark on both, with a favicon-scale version in the corner.

The marks

The selected mark first (picked 2026-07-05), then the rest roughly from most literal to most abstract. The favicon-scale copy in each corner is the honest test — a mark that survives 18 px earns app-icon duty.

Selected Pictogram

Dialectic

Thesis and antithesis: the agent asks, the market answers with a trend. Tails redrawn as part of each bubble's outline (v2) after owner feedback. The owner's pick — saved with its named lockup below.

Pictogram

The Examined Trade

"The unexamined trade is not worth making." A lens held over one green and one red candle — the only mark that admits losses exist.

Emblem

The Stoa

A temple whose middle column is a live candle — the market literally holds up the house. Institutional weight with a wink.

Pictogram

The Inquiry

The question mark is the method; its dot is re-cut as a green candlestick. Elenchus as price discovery — the whole app in one glyph.

Letterform

Phi

Philosophy's letter Φ with a candlestick for a spine — the wick runs clean through the circle. The most reducible mark of the set.

Pattern mark

Meander

The Greek key fret read literally: a staircase of higher highs, topped with a candle. Antiquity and an uptrend in the same stroke.

Mascot

Noctua

Athena's owl from the old drachma, re-minted as a coin; its chest feather is a small green candle. The friendliest of the twelve.

Letterform

Delta

Δ is change — the only thing the app trades on — framing a single candle under examination. Quiet, geometric, hard to date.

Emblem

The Laurel

An open wreath crowning the winning trade. Reads as an award badge — strong for reports and P&L summaries, less so as a favicon.

Monogram

Serpentine

The S of Socratic drawn as one continuous price path, ending on an emerald last-print dot. The most corporate-flexible option.

Pictogram

The Torch

Reason carried into the market — the flame doubles as a candle body. Simple silhouette, instantly legible at any size.

Wordmark

The Wordmark

Serif capitals with the period of "Socratic-Trade" re-cut as a candlestick — the domain name itself becomes the logo. Pairs with any mark above.
