## Decisions & Trade-offs

A soft 403 keeps the lane visible on Admin Connections but no longer pages or trips the hard breaker.  If Cboe blocks us permanently, the lane stays yellow.  Yahoo shares nothing with Cboe, so a real double outage still falls through to the CSV and FRED lanes and, past those, to the honest "unavailable" path.

The 403 cause is inferred (Cboe refusing the production host's egress); it was not probed from that host.
