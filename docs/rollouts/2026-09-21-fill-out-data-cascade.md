## 1. Context & Objective

Prior to this change, downstream trading and market analysis consumers received truncated quote data missing foundational metrics such as `prevClose`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `vwap`, `change`, `changePct`, and `bidSize`/`askSize`. Real-time endpoints for Finnhub (`/quote`) and Tiingo (`/iex`) were implemented in data provider libraries but omitted from `src/lib/quotes-cascade.ts`, leading to premature fallbacks onto 15-minute delayed Yahoo data. Furthermore, subsequent levels of the quote cascade completely discarded missing fields from authoritative broker quotes rather than coalescing them.

This work fills out the data cascade across all available multi-provider feeds (broker, Alpaca snapshots, Finnhub, Tiingo, Yahoo, and ROIC), introduces field-level coalescing with venue price authority preservation, enables dynamic metric recalculation in market aggregation, and asynchronously persists resolved fields to `symbol_field_latest`.

## 3. Decisions & Trade-offs

1. **Authoritative Venue Preservation:** Tradier paper sandbox quotes carry `venuePriceAuthoritative: true`. Subsequent cascade levels (such as Finnhub or Yahoo) must NEVER overwrite the execution venue's price, bid, ask, or timestamp. `mergeBrokerQuoteFields` strictly protects authoritative fields while allowing descriptive fields (`prevClose`, `vwap`, `open`, `high`, `low`) to backfill.
2. **Synthetic Flag Isolation:** Synthetic spread flags (`syntheticSpread`, `syntheticBid`, `syntheticAsk`) generated by fallback or keyless feeds are deliberately not merged if the existing quote already has a non-synthetic book, preventing synthetic flags from corrupting live NBBO books.
3. **Alpaca Snapshot Change Reference:** In `parseAlpacaSnapshot`, exchange intraday change and net change are calculated using `barClose ?? price` (the daily bar close `dailyBar.c`) rather than `price ?? barClose` (the last trade print `latestTrade.p`), because the daily bar close reflects the official exchange session close against `prevDailyBar.c`.
4. **Vendor Retirement Compliance:** Maintained strict adherence to the 2026-08-04 owner directive retiring FMP, QuiverQuant, and Unusual Whales direct vendor calls. Only approved feeds (broker, Alpaca, Finnhub, Tiingo, Yahoo, ROIC) were wired into the cascade.
